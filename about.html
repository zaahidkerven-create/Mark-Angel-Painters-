The business

Painting and maintenance contractors

Mark Angel Painters is a local property maintenance and professional painting contractor based in Randburg, Gauteng, specialising in residential and commercial painting, structural repairs and general home improvements.

The work is comprehensive interior and exterior contracting, and falls into three areas. Painting covers internal walls, ceiling restoration, exterior facades and boundary walls. Damp proofing and waterproofing covers rising damp and roof sealing, with surfaces prepared before any specialised weather-resistant paint is applied. Handyman and property maintenance covers minor structural repairs, fascia board replacements, high-pressure cleaning and general upkeep.

Because all three areas are undertaken by the same contractor, a fascia board may be replaced before it is decorated, and a damp wall treated before it is coated, without a second trade being engaged.
