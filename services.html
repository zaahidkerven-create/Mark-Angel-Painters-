Services

Three areas of work

The services below are those we undertake, set out by the three areas into which they fall. Each is given with its usual scope of works.

Residential & commercial painting

Decoration to residential and commercial property, internally and externally.

Interior wall painting

Full interior wall painting, with surfaces filled and sanded and floors and fittings covered before any coating begins.

Ceiling restoration

Stained and tired ceilings made good, sealed where they have marked, and recoated so the finish sits flat across the whole surface.

Exterior facades

Painting to external elevations. Loose material is removed and cracks made good before a weather-resistant coating is applied.

Boundary walls

Painting to perimeter and boundary walls. Blown plaster is patched and both faces coated to a consistent finish.

Damp proofing & waterproofing

Remedial work carried out ahead of any decoration applied over it.

Rising damp treatment

Damp drawn up through a wall is treated at the wall itself, rather than coated over and left to return through the new decoration.

Roof sealing

Coverings sealed at the point of ingress, with joints and edges given the same attention as open areas.

Surface preparation

Substrates prepared before any specialised coating is applied, so that the system bonds to sound material.

Weather-resistant coatings

Specialised weather-resistant paint applied over the prepared surface, specified for the elevation on which it is applied.

Handyman & property maintenance

General upkeep, undertaken under the same instruction as the decorating.

Minor structural repairs

Minor repairs to the fabric of the building, made good ahead of decoration.

Fascia board replacements

Deteriorated or damaged fascia boards removed and replaced, then finished in line with the remainder of the elevation.

High-pressure cleaning

Driveways, paving and walls washed down under pressure, either as works in their own right or as preparation ahead of coating.

General upkeep

Running maintenance to residential and commercial property, so that minor defects are addressed before they become substantial.

Contact us with details of the property and its condition, and we will advise which of the three areas the work falls into.
